Cadence vs Strong

Getting there, and what you do when you’re there · 30 October 2026

Short answer: they don’t compete. Strong is a workout logger for recording sets, reps and weights. Cadence is a gym habit app that counts your visits automatically. Strong helps you once you’re in the gym; Cadence helps you get there. Many people would be well served by both.

We make Cadence. Strong is a popular, well-regarded logger; check its App Store page for current features and price.

Choose Strong if

You go reliably and want to track progressive overload: adding weight or reps over time, seeing personal records, and following a programme.

Choose Cadence if

The hard part is turning up. Cadence asks for nothing in the gym, so it works even on the days you’d never open a logger. It nudges you on planned days and counts weeks, not days, so a rest day never breaks anything.

Using both

Cadence gets you through the door; Strong records the session. Neither duplicates the other. If you’re early in building the habit, start with Cadence alone and add a logger once going feels normal; see how long that usually takes.

Questions

Is Cadence a replacement for Strong?

No. Strong logs workouts; Cadence counts gym visits automatically to help you build the habit of going. They solve different problems and work together.

Do I need a workout logger to build a gym habit?

Not at first. Logging every set can be one more barrier when the hard part is turning up. Many people start by just tracking visits and add a logger later.

How Cadence helps. Cadence is a gym habit app for iPhone that notices when you arrive at your gym and counts the visit automatically. No workout logging. You set a weekly target and the days you mean to go, and your streak is counted in weeks, with one missed week a month forgiven automatically.
