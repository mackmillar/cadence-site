Gym apps compared: which kind do you actually need?

Workout loggers, habit trackers and gym habit apps · 30 October 2026

Short answer: if your problem is what to do in the gym, use a workout logger like Strong, Hevy or Fitbod. If your problem is getting there at all, use a habit app like Cadence. Plenty of people use one of each. Most “best gym apps” lists only cover the first kind.

We make Cadence, so read this with that in mind. We’ve tried to be fair about what the others do well, and about what Cadence doesn’t do. Features and prices change, so check each app’s App Store page.

The three kinds of gym app

Workout loggers

You record each exercise, set, rep and weight. They show your history and progress over time, and some suggest what to do next. Good for anyone who already goes and wants to get stronger in a structured way. They need input every session.

General habit trackers

You tick off any habit you choose: water, reading, the gym. Flexible, often beautifully designed, and good for tracking many habits in one place. Usually you mark the gym as done yourself, and most count daily streaks.

Gym habit apps

Built for the single question “did I go?”. Cadence notices when you arrive at your gym and counts the visit automatically, so there’s nothing to tick. It doesn’t record what you do inside.

Which should you use?

  • You go regularly and want to get stronger: a workout logger. Strong if you want it simple, Hevy if you like the social side, Fitbod if you want the plan written for you.
  • You want one app for lots of habits: a general habit tracker such as Streaks.
  • You keep meaning to go and don’t: Cadence. It’s built for the part before the workout, and it never asks you to log anything.
  • Both: Cadence to get you there, a logger once you’re inside. They don’t overlap.

What Cadence doesn’t do

No exercise library, no sets or reps, no workout plans, no calories, no social feed. It doesn’t track runs or home workouts; it counts visits to the gym you choose. If you need any of those, pair it with a logger. Read Cadence vs Strong and Cadence vs Streaks for the detail.

Questions

What is the best gym app?

It depends on the problem. For logging workouts and progress, apps like Strong, Hevy and Fitbod are popular. For building the habit of actually going, Cadence is a gym habit app for iPhone that notices when you arrive at your gym and counts the visit automatically. No workout logging.

Is there an app that tracks gym visits automatically?

Yes. Cadence uses your phone's geofencing to notice when you arrive at your chosen gym, and counts a stay of 20 minutes or more as a visit, even with the app closed.

Can I use Cadence with Strong or Hevy?

Yes. Cadence counts whether you went; a logger records what you did. They don't overlap.
