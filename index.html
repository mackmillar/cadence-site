Showing up is the whole battle.

Cadence notices when you get to the gym, so there is nothing to log.

What it does

You set a plan — how many times a week, which days, roughly when. Cadence watches for your arrival at the gym you chose and counts the visit itself. No check-in button, no sets, no reps, no calories.

What it doesn't do

It doesn't follow you around. Your phone's operating system watches a circle around your gym and tells the app when you arrive and leave; a visit is stored as a start time and an end time, never a place. It doesn't judge what you did once you were there, and it never uses guilt to get you back.

No tracking, no ads, no analytics. Cadence contains no advertising or analytics software of any kind.
