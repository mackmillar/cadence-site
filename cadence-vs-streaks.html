Cadence vs Streaks

Two iPhone habit apps, built for different jobs · 30 October 2026

Short answer: Streaks is a general habit tracker for a handful of daily habits. Cadence does one thing: it notices when you arrive at your gym and counts the visit, against a weekly target. If the gym is the habit you care about, Cadence fits it more closely. If you want one app for many habits, Streaks does more.

We make Cadence. Streaks is a well-made, long-established app, and we’ve tried to describe it fairly; check its App Store page for current features and price.

Where Streaks is better

Tracking several habits in one place, with a lot of flexibility over how each one counts. If “gym” is one line on a list of ten, Streaks is the better home for it.

Where Cadence is better

The gym specifically. Because it counts the visit when you arrive, there’s nothing to remember to tick, and it can’t be ticked from the sofa. The weekly target and forgiving weekly streak suit the gym, where rest days are part of the plan rather than a break in the chain. See how to build a gym habit for why that matters.

Can you use both?

Yes. Some people keep Streaks for everything else and let Cadence handle the gym.

Questions

Is Cadence or Streaks better for the gym?

For the gym alone, Cadence fits more closely: it counts visits automatically when you arrive and uses a weekly target. Streaks is better if you want to track many different habits in one app.

Does Streaks track gym visits automatically?

Streaks can complete some tasks from Apple Health data, such as workouts. Cadence counts a visit when you physically arrive at your chosen gym, using geofencing.

How Cadence helps. Cadence is a gym habit app for iPhone that notices when you arrive at your gym and counts the visit automatically. No workout logging. You set a weekly target and the days you mean to go, and your streak is counted in weeks, with one missed week a month forgiven automatically.
