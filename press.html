Press and facts

Everything you need to write about Cadence, in one place.

Cadence is a gym habit app for iPhone that notices when you arrive at your gym and counts the visit automatically. No workout logging.

In a paragraph

Most gym apps are for people who are already going. Cadence is for the part nobody helps with: turning up. You set a weekly target and the days you mean to go. After that there is nothing to do. Cadence notices when you arrive at your gym, counts the visit, and sends a short, kind nudge on the days you planned to go. It never asks what you did inside.

What makes it different

  • Nothing to log. No sets, reps, weights, calories or photos. Showing up is the whole battle, and the rest is the user’s business.
  • Automatic. The visit counts because you arrived, not because you remembered to tick something.
  • Forgiving by design. Weekly targets, weekly streaks and one forgiven week a month. Reminders are written to be read on a lock screen in a second, and none of them is a telling-off.
  • Private. It knows where your gym is, not where you are.
