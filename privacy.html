Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Cadence notices when you go to the gym so you don't have to log it. This policy says what that means for your data, and describes what the app actually does rather than what a template says.

The short version

  • Your visits are timestamps, not places. Cadence records that a visit happened and how long it lasted. It never stores where you were.
  • The only location Cadence keeps is the gym you chose.
  • There is no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind, and no third-party software collecting anything in the background.
  • Cadence never records what you did at the gym — no sets, reps, weights or calories. It only knows you showed up.

What Cadence stores

On your phone

  • Your plan: how many times a week, which days, your usual time.
  • Your gym: its name, address and coordinates, and the radius around it.
  • Your visits: a start time, an end time, a duration, and whether the visit was detected automatically. No coordinates are stored with a visit.
  • Which notifications were sent and whether you opened them, used to judge whether the nudges are worth sending.

On our server

So your history survives a lost phone, the above is also stored in a database hosted by Supabase, linked to an account identifier — along with your email address if you signed up with email. If you used Sign in with Apple or Google, we receive whatever that service gives us, typically an email address and an account identifier.

Every install is given an anonymous account identifier so backup works from the first visit. It is not linked to you personally unless you choose to sign in.

How Cadence uses location

Cadence asks for Always location access. This is the heart of the app and the reason it needs no manual check-in.

What that permission actually does: Cadence registers a circle around your gym with iOS or Android, and the operating system tells the app when you enter or leave it. Cadence does not follow you around. It does not track your position, record where you go, or build a history of your movements. It receives two events — arrived, left — and keeps the times.

Your phone's current position is read in one other place: to centre the map on the home screen. That position is held in memory while the screen is open, and is never stored or transmitted.

If you decline Always access, visits cannot be detected automatically.

Who else sees your data

  • Supabase hosts the database holding your account and synced history.
  • Google Places receives the text you type when searching for your gym, so it can suggest matches. Only the search text is sent — no identifier, no location.
  • Apple or Google, if you choose to sign in with them.

Nobody else. Your data is not sold, shared or used for advertising, and nothing is handed to data brokers or analytics providers.

Deleting everything

Open the account screen and choose Delete account. This removes your account and all of its data from the server, and clears the visits and plan stored on your phone. It is immediate and cannot be undone.

Deleting the app without deleting your account removes the local copy but leaves the server copy in place; delete the account first if you want both gone.

Children

Cadence is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what is collected, the date at the top will change and the app will say so before the change takes effect.
